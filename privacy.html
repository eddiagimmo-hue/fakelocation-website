Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

WhatsOn Family is operated by WhatsOn Family SAS. We are the data controller for personal data processed through our website and apps. You can reach us at privacy@whatsonfamily.com.

What We Collect

To run the service, we collect:

  • Account data: email, hashed password, language preference.
  • Tracked contacts: the phone numbers you choose to monitor and the labels you give them (e.g. "my son", "grandma").
  • Activity timestamps: the public WhatsApp "last seen" timestamps we observe for those numbers.
  • Billing: if you subscribe, Stripe collects your card details. We only store a Stripe customer ID and your invoice history.

What We Never Collect

  • WhatsApp messages, voice notes, calls, photos or media of any kind.
  • The tracked person's contact list, location or device data.
  • Your phone's address book.

Lawful Basis (GDPR Art. 6)

We process account and tracking data on the basis of the contract you sign up to (Art. 6.1.b GDPR). Aggregated analytics are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f) to improve the product, in a form that does not identify you.

Consent of the Tracked Person

You confirm at signup that you have informed every tracked contact that you are using WhatsOn Family to record their public WhatsApp last seen. For minors, parental authority is sufficient under most national laws, but we still recommend a transparent conversation. We provide a consent template inside the app.

Data Retention

Activity timestamps are retained for the duration of your active subscription plus 30 days. Free-tier users keep 24 hours of data on a rolling window. On account deletion, all personal data is erased within 30 days, except billing records we are legally required to keep (10 years in France).

Third-Party Processors

  • Stripe (payments, EU/US): privacy policy.
  • Amazon Web Services Ireland (hosting, EU).
  • Postmark (transactional email, US, GDPR DPA in place).

Your Rights

Under GDPR you may access, correct, port, restrict or delete your personal data, and object to processing. Write to privacy@whatsonfamily.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Access to production systems is limited to authorised engineers and logged.

Children

WhatsOn Family accounts can only be created by adults (18+). The product is designed for adults to track minors in their family with appropriate consent.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced by email at least 30 days before they take effect.
