Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What We Collect

FakeLocation operates primarily offline. The desktop and mobile apps do not collect, store, or transmit your GPS data, location history, or personal information to our servers.

When you purchase a Pro license, we collect your email address and payment information through Stripe. We do not store your credit card details — Stripe handles all payment processing securely.

License Validation

When you activate a Pro license, the app sends your license key and a device identifier (a hash, not your device serial number) to our server to verify your subscription status. No location data is transmitted.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (app version, OS version, feature usage counts) to improve the product. This data cannot be used to identify you.

Third-Party Services

Stripe (payments): subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Google Maps / OpenStreetMap (map tiles): your IP address is visible to the map tile provider when loading map data.

Data Retention

License data is retained for the duration of your subscription plus 90 days. You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@fakelocation.app.

Contact

For privacy questions: support@fakelocation.app
