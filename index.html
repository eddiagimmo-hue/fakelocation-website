The Family Guide to WhatsApp Last Seen Tracking

WhatsApp shows a small "last seen" timestamp at the top of every conversation. It is public information — anyone in your contact list who keeps the default privacy settings can see it. WhatsOn Family simply collects this public timestamp at regular intervals so that parents, partners and caretakers can build a calm, long-term view of when their loved ones are online.

Why families track WhatsApp online status

  • Bedtime routines: know whether your teenager is still texting after lights out.
  • School hours: spot WhatsApp activity during class time.
  • Reassurance: a single glance tells you a loved one is awake and reachable.
  • Coordination: long-distance couples or co-parents pick the best moment to call.

How is WhatsOn Family different from generic WhatsApp trackers?

Most apps that claim to track WhatsApp last seen are built around stalking adults. WhatsOn Family is the opposite: we ship an in-app consent template, we cap the number of tracked contacts, and we publish a transparency report every quarter. Our focus is family use — not surveillance.

What WhatsOn Family will never do

  • Read your messages, voice notes or media.
  • Access the tracked person's WhatsApp account.
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Bypass WhatsApp privacy settings: if the person hides their last seen, we respect that.

Is WhatsApp last seen tracking legal?

The last seen timestamp is public data exposed by WhatsApp itself. Reading public data is legal in most jurisdictions. However, depending on your country, tracking another adult without their consent can fall under privacy or harassment law. WhatsOn Family is designed and licensed for family use only — typically a parent monitoring a minor child, with the child informed.

Frequently Asked Questions

The honest answers about how WhatsOn Family works.

How does WhatsOn Family track WhatsApp last seen?
WhatsOn Family checks the public "last seen" timestamp that WhatsApp itself exposes for contacts who allow it. We never read messages and never access WhatsApp accounts. The tracked person must keep their "Last Seen" privacy setting open to Everyone or My Contacts.
Is WhatsOn Family legal and ethical?
WhatsOn Family is built for transparent family use. We require parents to inform minor children that their activity is being tracked, in line with GDPR and most national child-safety laws. The app is not intended for tracking adults without their knowledge.
Can I see WhatsApp messages with WhatsOn Family?
No. WhatsOn Family only logs when a contact appears online and when they were last seen. We have no access to messages, calls, photos or contacts inside WhatsApp.
Does WhatsOn Family work if the person hides their last seen?
No. If a WhatsApp user sets their Last Seen to "Nobody", no third-party tool can see it. Our app respects WhatsApp's privacy settings — there is no workaround.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Yes. WhatsOn Family is available as an iOS app, an Android app, and a web dashboard you can open from any browser. Your tracked contacts list syncs across devices.
Do I need to install anything on my kid's phone?
No. WhatsOn Family runs entirely on your side. We watch the public "last seen" data WhatsApp publishes for the contacts you add — no software on their device.
How much does WhatsOn Family cost?
WhatsOn Family is free for one tracked contact with 24 hours of history. Family Pro is 4.99 EUR per month or 29.99 EUR per year, with unlimited history, push alerts and weekly reports.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes. Cancel any time from the dashboard. You keep access until the end of the billing period. We also offer a 7-day no-questions-asked refund.
